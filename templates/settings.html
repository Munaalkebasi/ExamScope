Risk Score Calculation Formula (Python Engine)

Base Risk Formula:

Base Risk = (0.40 × Practice Gap + 0.35 × Confidence Gap + 0.25 × Review Decay) × 100

  • Practice Gap: (100 - practice_score) / 100
  • Confidence Gap: (100 - confidence) / 100
  • Review Decay: min(1.0, days_since_review / 12)
  • Exam Urgency Multiplier: As exam date approaches (≤ 14 days), weak topics receive up to +25% urgency amplifier.
Risk Bands: Critical (≥ 75), High (50–74), Moderate (25–49), Safe (< 25).
